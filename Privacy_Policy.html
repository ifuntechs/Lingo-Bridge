Privacy Policy

Lingo Bridge · Effective date: October 31, 2026

In short: Lingo Bridge does not ask you to create an account and does not collect, sell or share your personal information. Your language choices, scores and streak stay on your device.

1. Who we are

Lingo Bridge (“the app”) is a language-learning app published by iFuntechs (“we”, “us”), based in India. You can contact us at ifuntechs@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no sign-up or login, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

The app does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and we do not receive any data about how you use it.

3. Data stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves the following information locally on your phone:

  • the language you are learning and the language you already know;
  • your chosen level and topic;
  • your XP, daily progress and the dates you played (used for your daily streak);
  • the words and sentences you answered incorrectly (used for Review mode).

This information stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us or to any server. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone’s settings.

4. Text-to-speech (pronunciation)

When you tap a listen button, the app asks your phone’s built-in text-to-speech service to read a word or sentence aloud. That service is provided by your device or by the speech provider you have installed (for example, Google Text-to-Speech) and is governed by that provider’s own privacy policy. We do not receive or store the text that is spoken.

5. Permissions

The app does not request any special permissions.

6. Third parties

We do not share information with third parties because we do not collect it. If you installed the app from Google Play, Google may collect its own data about installs and updates under the Google Privacy Policy. This is separate from the app and outside our control.

7. Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no personal information is collected, there is nothing to share or sell. [If you declare the app as designed for children, add your Families Policy statement here.]

8. Data retention and deletion

Because all data is stored on your device, you are in control of it. To delete it, uninstall the app or choose Settings → Apps → Lingo Bridge → Storage → Clear data. We hold no copy that we could delete on your behalf.

9. Security

Your progress is stored in your app’s private storage on your device and is protected by your phone’s own security. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as access, correction or deletion. As we do not collect personal data, we hold nothing about you. If you have a question about your rights, email us and we will respond.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data (for example, by adding reminders, accounts, advertising or analytics), we will update this policy and the effective date above before the change takes effect.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email ifuntechs@gmail.com.
